title: "My transition to remote work has only reinforced loneliness"
date: 2025-06-03
---
I haven't had friends that I see in person several times a week since undergrad. Almost every day, I sit alone at my desk in my house working for eight hours, then I spend the rest of the day by myself or with my wife and dog.

Occasionally, I will go out to social events. But even after two years of living here, I don't think that I've found a group of people that I would classify as friends. But I don't think that not finding deep connections at these events is why I'm feeling this way. I felt more connected with people even when I was traveling overseas last week and I was in a crowd of tourists, where there were dozens of people around me at any given time all the time, even if I wasn't communicating with them. Sure, it was tiring to be around people like that all the time, but not once in those two weeks did I feel a sense of loneliness.

The difference between that experience and my average day-to-day experience at home is that I'm not casually around other people at home.

I never really noticed it until I realized how much happier I was around people – even people I don't know – while I was traveling overseas last week.
