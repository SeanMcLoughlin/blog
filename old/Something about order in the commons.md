# I

I live in a subdivision that is not yet fully built up. People are able to purchase lots of land from a developer to build a house on. My wife and I and I were some of the first people to build in this subdivision, so we've seen plenty of new houses get built while we live our day to day lives. Every day when we walk our dog, we get to see the progress made on each house. 

During these walks, we make a game of betting on what certain parts of houses are going to look like. What color will the house be? Based on the outline of the basement, what is the architecture style? We happened to get a look at the home owners when they went to go look at the property. Based on how they look, what do you think their tastes are?

It saddens me to report that, when we resolve these bets, we are often both wrong on our guesses and, simultaneously, both disappointed in the outcome. The house colors are cold and bland. The architecture style is lifelessly modern. And we have never successfully inferred anything from a glimpse at the owners.

From then on, our conversations as we pass the construction lots are "I can't believe they went with that color. What were they thinking with that room layout? They spent all that money to live in something as soulless as this?" A positive and hopeful experience has been dashed and turned into a joint reveling in misery.
